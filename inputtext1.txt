In 1909, the Hungarian writer Ferenc Molnar debuted his new play, Liliom, in Budapest. In the play, Liliom, a troubled and periodically violent young
carousel barker, falls in love with a woman named Julie. When Julie becomes pregnant, Liliom attempts a robbery to support his burgeoning
family, but the robbery is a disaster, and Liliom dies. He ends up in purgatory for sixteen years, after which he is allowed a single day to visit
his now-teenaged daughter, Louise.
